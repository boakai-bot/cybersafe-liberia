Why Cybersecurity Matters in Liberia

Technology connects us to education, business, government services, banking, communication, and communities. But as our digital lives grow, cyber threats also continue to evolve.

Cybersecurity awareness helps individuals and organizations recognize threats and make safer decisions online.

Common Cyber Threats

Understanding common threats is the first step toward protecting yourself.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick you into revealing passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data.

🔑

Password Attacks

Attackers may attempt to guess, steal, reuse, or crack passwords to gain access to accounts.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software can damage devices, steal information, spy on users, or disrupt systems.

📱

Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people into taking actions that compromise their security.

10 Cybersecurity Safety Tips

01

Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts.

02

Enable multi-factor authentication whenever possible.

03

Do not click suspicious links or unexpected attachments.

04

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

05

Be careful when using public Wi-Fi networks.

06

Back up important files regularly.

07

Verify unexpected requests for money or sensitive information.

08

Review your privacy and security settings.

09

Lock your devices when you are not using them.

10

Report suspicious activity instead of ignoring it.

Stop. Think. Verify.

Before clicking a link, opening an attachment, sending money, or sharing sensitive information, take a moment to verify the request.

Ask yourself:

  • Do I know the sender?
  • Was I expecting this message?
  • Is the website address correct?
  • Is the request creating unnecessary urgency?
  • Can I verify the request another way?

Think You Have Been Hacked?

  1. Disconnect the affected device from the network if appropriate.
  2. Change compromised passwords from a trusted device.
  3. Enable multi-factor authentication.
  4. Check your accounts for unauthorized activity.
  5. Preserve relevant evidence and report the incident to the appropriate organization or authority.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you encounter suspicious online activity, phishing, fraud, malware, or another cyber incident, document what happened and report it through the appropriate organizational or national channel.
